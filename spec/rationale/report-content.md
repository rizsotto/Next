# Report content

## Context

Reports serve two goals: improving the flow, and evaluating performance.
The first needs no names and the second does, so they carry different risk.

Speed is not quality. A worker who rushes looks better than one who solves
the hard cases, and different services do not compare at all. Measuring
staff may also need union or works council approval in some markets.

## Decision

Keep two reports. The flow report names nobody. The worker report compares
workers only within a service, has no overall ranking, and is off until the
office admin turns it on.

## Consequences

An office can use the flow report with no decision about staff data. The
worker report still needs care: its numbers are a starting point for a
conversation, not a verdict.

Export and charts are out of the MVP. Revisit if report readers ask for them.
